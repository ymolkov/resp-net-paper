Respiratory neurophysiology

From cellular synaptic input profiles to population-level respiratory circuitry

Yaroslav I. Molkov, Ilya A. Rybak, and Jeffrey C. Smith

Explore the circuit Read the paper

The approach

From individual cells to a population circuit

Discharge patterns tell us when respiratory neurons fire. To ask which populations might supply their synaptic inputs, we compared the timing of excitation and inhibition in 150 cells with five independently recorded patterns of population activity.

A

Reconstruct each cell’s inputs

For each neuron, we estimated how excitation and inhibition change over a breath. We grouped neurons by when they fire.

B

Ask which sources are needed

We treated the same populations as possible sources. If we leave one out, can the others still reproduce the timing of the input across cells?

C

Assemble the circuit

A solid connection identifies a source the recordings distinguish. Dashed connections show alternatives: different sources can produce similar inputs.

Circuit interpretation

Excitation links populations active in different phases.

Both expiratory populations received inspiratory excitation alongside inhibition. In pre-I/I neurons, excitation with an augmenting-expiratory pattern rose as post-inspiratory inhibition declined. These inputs could help recruit the next inspiration.
